## What each model actually gets you

### Litter-Robot EVO — the small-space, one-cat pick

The EVO is the smallest box Whisker has made: 27.6 inches tall on a 20.6 x 24.3-inch footprint, which is roughly the floor space of a large traditional pan. Interior headroom is 14.5 inches, and the weight range tops out at 19 pounds.

It is also the only model that gives up real features to hit $599. There's no litter-level sensing, so you check the globe with your eyes. Waste-level sensing is limited to "the drawer is full" rather than showing you where the level sits. There's no LCD screen — you get indicator lights. There's no WasteID. And the EVO doesn't accept the LitterHopper, Whisker's auto-refill accessory.

For one or two cats under 19 pounds in an apartment, none of that matters much. If either cat is a big Maine Coon or you're already thinking about a third cat, the savings disappear fast.

### Litter-Robot 4 — the proven middle

Released in 2022, still the model Whisker calls its bestseller, and still the one most reviewers reach for first. It handles up to four cats at 3–25 pounds with 16.5 inches of headroom, adds litter-level and waste-level sensing, and works with the LitterHopper. Whisker's site points to more than 13,000 reviews on this model, and the third-party comparisons generally treat it as the low-risk choice because the hardware has been in homes long enough that the failure modes are known.

One practical catch: the Litter-Robot 4 is designed for hard flooring. On carpet you need the Carpet Tray add-on, which is sold separately. The EVO has a carpet tray built in, and the 5-series boxes sit on carpet without extra parts.

### Litter-Robot 5 — bigger drawer, smarter waste handling

Where the 5 separates itself: a slightly larger globe, an extra-large waste drawer good for up to about 10 days with a single cat, support for cats from 3 to 30 pounds, and space for a fifth cat. The control panel swaps indicator lights for a full-color LCD, which means you can read the status of the unit without opening the app.

The headline feature is WasteID, which distinguishes urine from feces and changes the cleaning behavior accordingly — it waits so urine can clump properly, and cycles immediately on solid waste for odor control. Worth being precise here: the sensing and the cycling logic work on the device, but the detailed breakdown in the app of how much of each your cat produced sits behind the Whisker+ subscription.

### Litter-Robot 5 Pro — cameras, and a narrower reason to buy

The 5 Pro adds two night-vision cameras to the Litter-Robot 5. One faces outward and uses facial recognition to identify which cat is walking in; the other watches the litter bed. The result is individualized tracking rather than weight-based guessing, which matters in households where two cats weigh within a pound of each other. It's also the model that works with the Whisker PetTag, a $29 RFID collar tag that helps the box tell similar-looking cats apart and doubles as a QR-code ID if your cat gets out.

Two honest caveats. The software side isn't magic — Whisker's own support documentation says the facial recognition can struggle with cats that look alike, which is exactly why the PetTag exists. And live video plus saved footage requires the Whisker+ subscription. Third-party reviews land in a similar place: the camera is worth the extra $100 if you're tracking a senior or chronically ill cat, or if weight-based ID keeps getting confused. For a one-cat household it's a nice-to-have sitting on a $899 box.

## What's free, what costs extra

The Whisker app itself is free on every model and covers the basics: on-demand cycles, alerts when the drawer needs emptying, litter and waste levels on supported models, weight and usage history for the last seven days, and cat profiles.

Whisker+ is optional and runs $7.99 a month or $79.90 a year. It adds longer trend history, a daily recap, deeper bathroom-behavior analysis, video recording, and visual cat identification on the 5 Pro. Whisker has said publicly that it is not moving previously free features behind the paywall — Whisker+ is meant to add new ones.

## Five things people miss before ordering

**1. Litter type is not optional.** The sifting mechanism needs clumping litter. Absorbent, non-clumping, wood pellet, and newspaper-based litters are explicitly out of bounds. Crystal or translucent litter can also throw off the litter-level and drawer-full sensors.

**2. The footprint is permanent.** The Litter-Robot 4 and both 5-series models sit on a 22 x 27-inch footprint and stand about 30 inches tall. That's not something you slide into a cupboard between uses, and the unit can't be pushed against a wall or into a corner — it needs clearance.

**3. "Self-cleaning" isn't "self-maintaining."** You still empty the drawer (weekly-ish for one cat, twice a week for two average cats), replace carbon filters and liners, and deep clean the globe every one to three months. The EVO runs about every six to seven days for a single cat.

**4. Multi-cat households often need two boxes.** Whisker rates the Litter-Robot 4 for up to four cats and the 5 and 5 Pro for up to five, but cat behavior doesn't follow spec sheets. With three or more cats, many owners end up with a second unit so nobody has to queue.

**5. Old litter box stays in place first.** The standard advice — and the thing most likely to turn a return into a refund — is to put the Litter-Robot next to the existing box, leave it powered off for a day or two, and add a scoop of used litter for the familiar scent. Pulling the old box the same day is how acclimation fails.

## So which one should you buy?

Skip the spec-sheet paralysis and work backwards from your household:

- **One or two cats under 19 lb, tight floor space, budget-conscious:** Litter-Robot EVO. You're giving up litter-level sensing and LitterHopper support and getting a $100–$200 discount for it.
- **Two to four cats, mixed sizes, want the safest bet:** Litter-Robot 4. Mature hardware, wide opening, LitterHopper compatibility, and it's the model with the deepest review base.
- **Four cats, a cat over 25 lb, or you genuinely want bathroom-type data:** Litter-Robot 5. The extra $100 over the 4 buys a larger drawer, a wider weight range and a fifth cat's worth of capacity.
- **Similar-looking cats, or you're monitoring a cat with a chronic condition:** Litter-Robot 5 Pro. This is the only configuration where the camera earns its price.

Third-party reviewers mostly land on the same split. Wired's tester, who has worked through close to 20 automatic boxes, still points back to the Litter-Robot 4 while naming the 5 as the best pick for most people and the EVO as the budget option. Petful's comparison carves the same lines: EVO for small one- or two-cat homes, the 5 for five cats or big cats, and the 5 Pro specifically for households that need to distinguish cats by face rather than weight.

The one area where everyone agrees is that this is an appliance, not a household employee. It removes scooping. It doesn't remove maintenance, and it won't diagnose your cat. If you want the habit data to mean something, you'll be reading it yourself or paying $7.99 a month to have Whisker+ summarize it.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does Whisker still sell the Litter-Robot 3?**
No. The Litter-Robot 3 Connect has been discontinued. If you find one deeply discounted, you're buying hardware without QuietSift cycling, without the weight scale, and without the app features the current models share.

**Can one Litter-Robot serve five cats?**
Whisker rates the 5 and 5 Pro for up to five. In practice, a five-cat home should expect to empty the drawer every four to five days rather than weekly, and weight-based identification can misattribute visits when two cats weigh within a pound of each other. That's the exact scenario the PetTag was made for.

**Will it work on carpet?**
The EVO and the 5-series models can sit on carpet as-is. The Litter-Robot 4 needs the Carpet Tray accessory, sold separately.

**Do I have to subscribe to Whisker+?**
No. The app is free and stays free. Whisker+ is an optional upgrade, and it's required only for the deeper insights and, on the 5 Pro, for video.

**What if my cat refuses to use it?**
You have 90 days to return it for a refund of the purchase price; you pay return shipping. That makes the trial the single best reason to order direct rather than through a marketplace listing, where the return policy is somebody else's.
